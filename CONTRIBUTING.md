# Contributing

Thanks for wanting to help.
There is one rule.

## Ask before you code

Comment on the issue and wait for a maintainer to assign it to you; on issues labeled `help-wanted` or `good-first-issue`, comment `assign me` and you are assigned right away.
Pull requests from anyone not assigned to the issue are closed automatically, and an assignment is released after 14 days without activity.

No issue yet? Open one first, through one of the [issue forms](https://github.com/hotcodepush-team/.github/tree/main/.github/ISSUE_TEMPLATE).

## Your pull request

The [pull request template](https://github.com/hotcodepush-team/.github/blob/main/.github/PULL_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.md) is the checklist.
Two of its items are checked automatically: the title has to be a conventional commit, and the body has to link the issue with `Closes #`, `Fixes #` or `Resolves #`.

## License

There is no CLA.
Every public repository is MIT, and opening a pull request contributes your change under that same license — MIT in, MIT out.

## Conduct

The [Code of Conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md) applies everywhere in this organization.
