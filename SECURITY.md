# Security Policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Report it privately, never as a public issue.

Two ways, both reaching the same people:

- GitHub's [private vulnerability reporting](https://docs.github.com/en/code-security/security-advisories/guidance-on-reporting-and-writing-information-about-vulnerabilities/privately-reporting-a-security-vulnerability), through the **Security** tab of the affected repository.
- Email to [security@hotcodepush.com](mailto:security@hotcodepush.com).

Please include what you found, the version or URL it affects, and the steps to reproduce it.
We answer every report, and we credit reporters who want to be credited.

## Scope

Anything in the `hotcodepush-team` organization and anything served from `hotcodepush.com`.
